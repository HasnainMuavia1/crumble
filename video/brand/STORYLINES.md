# Crumble brand film — storylines

1. **One cookie, every craving (picked).** Object-to-object chain on Crumble's own colour worlds. A Double Chocolate cookie slams onto the blue, cracks open, falls into soft-serve, and the film rides colour waves through shakes, Crumble Cold, ten real cookies and the Pot, ending with the cookie mark landing as the "u" in the crumble wordmark. Cut on the bar of a 132 BPM funk track.
2. **The pull.** One continuous macro take: the camera never cuts, it follows the molten chocolate strand as it stretches between two halves and travels through every product. Slower, sensual, soft-serve and syrup sounds.
3. **Crumble across Pakistan.** A blue Crumble box travels a dashed route through seven cities; at each stop the box opens on a different product. A road-trip montage with city names as kinetic type.

Picked 1: it uses every real asset, shows the whole menu, and the bar-cut rhythm suits a 30 s social film.
